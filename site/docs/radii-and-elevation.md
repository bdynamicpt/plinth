# Radii and elevation

Depth comes from hairline borders and small surface steps, not shadows. Shadows are only for
what floats above the page.

## Radii

Named by what they round, so a brand can square its controls without touching its pills: 6 px on
controls, 10 px on containers, a pill for badges and avatars.

## Elevation

Shadows for what floats: cards that lift (1), popovers and menus (2), sheets and dialogs (3). Dark
has stronger shadows, because a soft one vanishes on a dark surface.
