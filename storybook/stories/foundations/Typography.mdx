# Typography

Geist and Geist Mono by default, self-hosted (load `@bdynamic/plinth/fonts.css`; Laravel and
Filament get them from the Composer package). Use the sans for interface text and the mono for
metadata: handles, hashes, times and key hints. A project sets its own typefaces by overriding
`--plinth-font-family-body` and `--plinth-font-family-mono`.

## Scale

UI text is 14px, with 13px and 12px below it, and reading text is 15px. Larger sizes are for
headings. **5xl and 6xl are display sizes for marketing pages only** ([ADR 3](https://github.com/bdynamicpt/plinth/blob/main/docs/adr/0003-visual-direction.md)):
inside an app the scale stops at 4xl (36px). Pair them with the `display` line height and the
`tighter` letter spacing, and step them down on smaller screens: 4xl on a phone, 5xl from
768px, 6xl from 1280px.
