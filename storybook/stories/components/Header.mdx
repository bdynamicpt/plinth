# Header

The top bar of the phone shell: a leading action (back or the menu), the page's title, and a few
trailing actions. It sticks to the top of the page while the content scrolls under it.

## Markup

<Source
  language="html"
  dark
  code={`<header class="plinth-header">
  <div class="plinth-header-leading">
    <button type="button" class="plinth-button plinth-icon-button" data-variant="ghost"
      data-size="md" aria-label="Menu" commandfor="menu" command="show-modal">
      <span class="plinth-icon" data-size="md" aria-hidden="true"><svg>…</svg></span>
    </button>
  </div>
  <h1 class="plinth-header-title">Inbox</h1>
  <div class="plinth-header-actions">
    <button type="button" class="plinth-button plinth-icon-button" data-variant="ghost"
      data-size="md" aria-label="Search">…</button>
    <button type="button" class="plinth-button plinth-icon-button" data-variant="ghost"
      data-size="md" aria-label="More">…</button>
  </div>
</header>`}
/>

- **`plinth-header`**, the root, is a `<header>`; a project's classes and attributes land on it.
  At the top of the page, outside `<main>`, `<article>` and `<section>`, it is the page's banner
  landmark, so a page has one header.
- **`plinth-header-leading`** is optional and holds one action, usually an
  [icon button](?path=/docs/components-icon-button--docs): a **back** link (with `mirror`, so
  its arrow points the other way in right-to-left text) or a **menu** button that opens the
  app's menu sheet with `commandfor` and `command="show-modal"`. The app supplies that
  [sheet](?path=/docs/components-sheet--docs); the On a page story below has one.
- **`plinth-header-title`** is an `<h1>`, the page's heading, and is required. It stays on one
  line and ends in an ellipsis when it doesn't fit.
- **`plinth-header-actions`** is optional and holds the trailing actions: two or three icon
  buttons at most. Put the rest behind a "More" button that opens a menu.

The leading part and the actions are printed only when they have content. Every icon button needs
its own name (`label`) in the page's language. The header needs `button.css` and `icon.css` for
them.

The header has no variants or states of its own: what its actions do is the app's, through a
link, a `commandfor`, `wire:click` or `@click` on the icon button.

## Size and the safe area

The header is **`--plinth-header-height`** tall (56px), a component token a project can tune.
On a phone that draws under the status bar and the notch (`viewport-fit=cover` in the viewport
meta) it adds `env(safe-area-inset-top)` above that, and its inline padding clears the notch on a
phone held sideways.

## Sticky

The header is `position: sticky` at the top, on the **`--plinth-layer-sticky`** z-index: above
the page's content, below sheets and menus, which sit in the browser's top layer. It sticks
within its scrolling parent, so put it at the top of the page, not inside a box that scrolls on
its own.

Content scrolls under the header, so a focused element or the target of an in-page link can land
behind it. Set `scroll-padding-block-start` on the page's scrolling element to keep them clear:

<Source
  language="css"
  dark
  code={`html {
  scroll-padding-block-start: calc(var(--plinth-header-height) + env(safe-area-inset-top));
}`}
/>
