# Pill row

A row of pills for filters, segments or the sub-pages of a section. Each pill has a label and an
optional count. On a phone the row scrolls sideways, with no visible scrollbar, and snaps to the
pills; from 768px it wraps onto more lines.

## Filters or navigation

The row and each pill are their own components, so the app's attributes and listeners
(`wire:click`, `x-on:click`, `@click`) land on the pill (ADR 2).

- **Filters** are buttons. A button pill is a toggle and always prints `aria-pressed`, `"true"`
  or `"false"`, so a screen reader announces which ones are on. The row is a
  `<div role="group">`; name it with **`label`** (_Status_), or point `aria-labelledby` at a
  heading. One pill or several can be on: the app decides.
- **Navigation** pills are links. Set **`nav`** on the row to make it a `<nav>`, give each pill
  an `href`, and set `selected` on the one for the page being shown, which prints
  `aria-current="page"`.

<Canvas of={PillRowStories.Navigation} />

Tab moves through the pills, and Space or Enter toggles a filter. The selected pill takes the
selection tint and is filled, where the others are outlined, so it doesn't rely on colour alone;
in forced colours it takes the system highlight.

## Disabled

A disabled button pill gets `disabled`; a disabled link pill loses its `href` and reads as a
disabled link (`role="link"`, `aria-disabled="true"`).

## Many pills

The row scrolls on a phone and snaps each pill to its start. Its own small padding keeps the
focus ring and the 40px touch target of the first and last pill inside the scrolling area. To
let the row run to the edges of the screen, give it the page's gutter as `padding-inline` and the
same negative `margin-inline`.

## Tuning

A pill is `--plinth-pill-height` (32px) tall, and keeps a 40px hit area on a touch screen. A
project changes the height in its tokens.

## In Blade and Vue

<Source
  language="html"
  dark
  code={`<x-plinth::pill-row label="Status">
    @foreach ($statuses as $status)
        <x-plinth::pill
            :count="$status->count"
            :selected="$filter === $status->value"
            wire:click="$set('filter', '{{ $status->value }}')"
        >{{ $status->label }}</x-plinth::pill>
    @endforeach
</x-plinth::pill-row>

<PlinthPillRow label="Project sections" nav>
  <PlinthPill href="/overview" :selected="route.name === 'overview'">Overview</PlinthPill>
  <PlinthPill href="/tasks" :count="8" :selected="route.name === 'tasks'">Tasks</PlinthPill>
</PlinthPillRow>`}
/>

The markup contract is at the top of `packages/css/src/components/pill-row.css`.
