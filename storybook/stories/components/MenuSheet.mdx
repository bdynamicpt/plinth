# Menu sheet

The app shell's menu: a [sheet](?path=/docs/components-sheet--docs) with the signed-in user, the
navigation and the account actions. The header's menu button opens it. On a phone it slides up
from the bottom; from 768px up it is a full-height panel on the inline-end edge.

The menus on this page stay closed until you press their button; open a story on its own to see
it open.

## Markup

  code={`<button type="button" class="plinth-button plinth-icon-button" data-variant="ghost"
  data-size="md" aria-label="Menu" commandfor="menu" command="show-modal">…</button>

<dialog id="menu" class="plinth-sheet plinth-menu-sheet" data-placement="end" closedby="any"

      commandfor="menu" command="close">…</button>
  </div>
  <div class="plinth-sheet-body">
    <a class="plinth-list-row plinth-menu-sheet-user" href="/profile">
      <span class="plinth-list-row-leading">
        <span class="plinth-avatar" data-size="lg" aria-hidden="true">…</span>
      </span>
      <span class="plinth-list-row-content">
        <span class="plinth-list-row-title">Sam Lee</span>
        <span class="plinth-list-row-description">sam@example.com</span>
      </span>
      <span class="plinth-list-row-trailing">…</span>
    </a>
    <nav class="plinth-menu-sheet-nav" aria-labelledby="menu-title">
      <div class="plinth-list-card">…</div>
    </nav>
    <div class="plinth-menu-sheet-account">
      <div class="plinth-list-card">…</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</dialog>`}
/>

- **`plinth-menu-sheet`** sits on a sheet's root, always `data-placement="end"`, with no
  footer. Everything the sheet does holds: the browser traps focus, closes it on Escape or a tap
  on the backdrop, and returns focus to the menu button. Give it an **`id`** and point the
  header's menu button at it with `commandfor` and `command="show-modal"`; Blade and Vue
  generate one when you leave it out, but the button needs to know it.
- The **title** is _Menu_ by default; set it, and **`close-label`** (Blade) or
  **`closeLabel`** (Vue), in the page's language. It names the sheet and the navigation.
- **`plinth-menu-sheet-user`** is optional: a [list row](?path=/docs/components-list-row--docs)
  with a decorative avatar, the user's name and an optional detail line (an email or a role).
  Give it a **`user-href`** and it links to the profile, with a chevron.
- **`plinth-menu-sheet-nav`** is a `<nav>` named by the title. It holds
  [list cards](?path=/docs/components-list-card--docs) of link rows, one card or several with
  headings. The sheet's title is an `<h2>`, so the cards' headings are `<h3>`
  (`heading-level="h3"`).
- **`plinth-menu-sheet-account`** is optional: a list card of account actions, such as
  _Settings_ and _Sign out_. In the full-height panel it sits on the bottom edge.

The menu needs `sheet.css`, `list-row.css`, `list-card.css`, `avatar.css`, `button.css` and
`icon.css`.

## The current page

The app marks the row of the page being shown as current (`current` on the list row, which
prints `aria-current="page"`) and keeps it up to date. The menu remembers nothing. A link that
navigates closes the menu with the page; one that stays on the page (a Livewire or Vue router
link) closes it with `close()` or `command="close"`.

<Canvas of={MenuSheetStories.Grouped} />

## Signing out

Signing out changes state, so it is a button row, never a link: one with `wire:click` or
`@click`, or one with `type="submit"` and `form="…"` that posts a form elsewhere on the page.

## Long menus

The body scrolls as one, and the account follows the navigation.

## In Blade and Vue

<Source
  language="html"
  dark
  code={`<x-plinth::menu-sheet id="menu" :user-name="$user->name" :user-detail="$user->email" user-href="/profile">
    <x-plinth::list-card heading-level="h3">
        <li><x-plinth::list-row href="/inbox" :current="request()->routeIs('inbox')">Inbox</x-plinth::list-row></li>
        <li><x-plinth::list-row href="/projects" :current="request()->routeIs('projects*')">Projects</x-plinth::list-row></li>
    </x-plinth::list-card>
    <x-slot:account>
        <x-plinth::list-card heading-level="h3">
            <li><x-plinth::list-row href="/settings">Settings</x-plinth::list-row></li>
            <li><x-plinth::list-row button wire:click="signOut">Sign out</x-plinth::list-row></li>
        </x-plinth::list-card>
    </x-slot:account>
</x-plinth::menu-sheet>

<PlinthMenuSheet id="menu" :user-name="user.name" :user-detail="user.email" @close="onClose">
  <PlinthListCard heading-level="h3">
    <li><PlinthListRow href="/inbox" :current="route.name === 'inbox'">Inbox</PlinthListRow></li>
  </PlinthListCard>
  <template #account>
    <PlinthListCard heading-level="h3">
      <li><PlinthListRow button @click="signOut">Sign out</PlinthListRow></li>
    </PlinthListCard>
  </template>
</PlinthMenuSheet>`}
/>

In Livewire, put `wire:ignore.self` on the menu sheet, as on any sheet, so a re-render keeps it
open. In Vue, a ref's `showModal()` and `close()` open and close it, and it emits `close`.

The markup contract is at the top of `packages/css/src/components/menu-sheet.css`.
