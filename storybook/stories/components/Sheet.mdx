# Sheet

A modal panel for a task that sits on top of the page: filters, a short form, a confirmation, a
list of options. On a phone it slides up from the bottom; from 768px up it shows as a dialog in
the middle of the screen, or as a full-height panel on the side.

The sheets on this page stay closed until you press their button. Under the first one, **At every
width** shows it open on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.

## Markup

<Source
  language="html"
  dark
  code={`<button type="button" class="plinth-button" data-variant="secondary" data-size="md"
  commandfor="filters" command="show-modal">Filters</button>

<dialog id="filters" class="plinth-sheet" data-placement="center" closedby="any"
  aria-labelledby="filters-title" aria-describedby="filters-description">
  <div class="plinth-sheet-header">
    <h2 id="filters-title" class="plinth-sheet-title">Filters</h2>
    <p id="filters-description" class="plinth-sheet-description">Only the items that match are shown.</p>
    <button type="button" class="plinth-button plinth-icon-button plinth-sheet-close"
      data-variant="ghost" data-size="md" aria-label="Close" commandfor="filters" command="close">
      <span class="plinth-icon" data-size="md" aria-hidden="true"><svg>…</svg></span>
    </button>
  </div>
  <div class="plinth-sheet-body">…</div>
  <div class="plinth-sheet-footer">…</div>
</dialog>`}
/>

- **`plinth-sheet`**, the root, is a `<dialog>`; a project's classes and attributes land on it.
  It needs an **`id`**: the close button's `commandfor` and the `aria-labelledby` point at it.
  Blade and Vue generate one when you leave it out, but pass your own when a button elsewhere
  opens the sheet.
- **`plinth-sheet-title`** is an `<h2>` and the sheet's accessible name. It is required.
- **`plinth-sheet-description`** is optional, and becomes the sheet's accessible description
  (`aria-describedby`). Leave it out when the body says enough.
- **`plinth-sheet-close`** is an [icon button](?path=/docs/components-icon-button--docs), and
  is always there. Set its name with **`close-label`** (Blade) or **`closeLabel`** (Vue) in the
  page's language; it is "Close" by default. The sheet needs `button.css` and `icon.css`.
- **`plinth-sheet-body`** holds the content and scrolls when it is taller than the screen.
- **`plinth-sheet-footer`** is optional, usually the sheet's actions. On a phone they share its
  width; from 768px up they sit at the end.

## Placement

**`data-placement`** sets how the sheet shows from 768px up. On a phone every sheet is a bottom
sheet.

| Value              | From 768px up                                                         |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `center` (default) | a dialog in the middle of the screen, `--plinth-sheet-width` wide     |
| `end`              | a full-height panel on the end edge, `--plinth-sheet-side-width` wide |

An unknown value falls back to `center`. The end edge is the right in left-to-right text and the
left in right-to-left text.

## Opening and closing

The sheet is a native modal dialog (ADR 2), so the browser does the work: it puts the sheet above
everything, dims the page behind it and makes it inert, keeps focus inside, closes on Escape and
returns focus to the button that opened it. Open is the dialog's own `open` attribute, never a
class.

- **Open it** with a button that has `commandfor="<id>" command="show-modal"`, with no script, or
  call `showModal()` on the dialog. Don't add `open` to the markup: that shows the dialog without
  making it modal.
- **Close it** with the close button, Escape, or any button with `command="close"` (an Apply or
  Cancel in the footer). A `<form method="dialog">` inside closes it on submit too.
- **Tapping the backdrop** closes it where the browser supports `closedby="any"`. Safari doesn't
  yet, which is why the close button is always there. Set `closedby="closerequest"` on a sheet
  that must not close on a stray tap, such as a form with unsaved changes.
- When it closes, the dialog fires **`close`**.

<x-plinth::sheet id="filters" title="Filters" description="Only the items that match are shown.">
…
<x-slot:footer>
<x-plinth::button variant="secondary" wire:click="resetFilters">Reset</x-plinth::button>
<x-plinth::button commandfor="filters" command="close">Apply</x-plinth::button>
</x-slot:footer>
</x-plinth::sheet>`}
/>

From Alpine, `x-ref="filters"` on the sheet and `$refs.filters.showModal()`.

**In Livewire,** a re-render would morph the open dialog back to the server's markup and drop its
`open` attribute, closing it mid-task. Put `wire:ignore.self` on the sheet (or keep it outside
the component that re-renders), and always pass an `id`: a generated one changes on every
render. Blade needs a `title` and throws without one.

In Vue, the component exposes **`showModal()`** and **`close()`**, and emits **`close`** (with
the dialog's native event) whenever the dialog closes. It needs a `title` and throws without one:

  language="html"
  dark
  code={`<PlinthButton variant="secondary" @click="filters.showModal()">Filters</PlinthButton>

<PlinthSheet ref="filters" id="filters" title="Filters" @close="applyFilters">
  …
  <template #footer>
    <PlinthButton commandfor="filters" command="close">Apply</PlinthButton>
  </template>
</PlinthSheet>`}
/>

## Scrolling

The body scrolls on its own, and scrolling past its end doesn't move the page
(`overscroll-behavior: contain`). While a sheet is open the page under it doesn't scroll at all
(`html:has(.plinth-sheet:modal)`), and `scrollbar-gutter: stable` keeps the page from shifting
when its scrollbar goes. On a phone the sheet leaves a gap at the top, so it reads as a sheet
over the page, and its last part clears the home indicator; a side panel clears it, and the
notch on a phone held sideways, too.

The body resets the margins of its first and last child, so its padding is its edge.

## The on-screen keyboard

On a phone the sheet sits on the bottom edge. Its height is capped by `100dvh`, but iOS lays the
keyboard over the page instead of resizing it, so a field low in the sheet can end up under the
keyboard. For a sheet with a form:

- set `interactive-widget=resizes-content` in the page's viewport meta, where the browser
  supports it, so the viewport (and the sheet) shrinks above the keyboard:
  `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, interactive-widget=resizes-content">`;
- keep the fields near the top of the sheet, with its actions in the footer.

## Motion

The sheet slides up on a phone, fades and rises a little as a dialog, and slides in from the side
as a panel, on `--plinth-duration-normal` with the enter and exit easings. Under
`prefers-reduced-motion` the durations drop to 0ms, so it simply appears.

## Depth

A sheet floats above the page, so it is one of the few places with a shadow (ADR 3):
`--plinth-elevation-3`, on `--plinth-color-surface-raised` with a hairline edge, over a
backdrop in `--plinth-color-overlay`.
